RaceGOW General Rules
Code of Ethics
It would be impossible for myself and the community to perfectly police every aspect of this game so I must rely on the honesty of all the participants to keep the game fair and free of any type of intentional manipulation or cheating. You are absolutely not allowed to attempt to gain an advantage in any way, including by not limited to: building the track not to the required specifications, speeding up your DVR or manipulating the timing in any way, cutting together non-consecutive laps, having another pilot or AI/computer or anything other than yourself flying your drone, etc etc. There is a ZERO TOLERANCE POLICY for cheating. If you are caught cheating you will immediately be removed from the game and you will forfeit any prizes you may have won. Please don’t be an asshole, DO NOT Cheat.

Season Scoring

RaceGOW Season Long Scoring:

Main Leaderboard aka Open Class:
1. Three separate scoring systems:
2. Standard Overall Rank Scoring System
1. The overall rank from each track is added up
2. The two lowest ranking (highest position) track is dropped
3. The lowest total score wins
4. Ties broken by dropped score
3. Scoring System based on Overall Time
1. The 3-lap time from each track is added up
2. A pilot must participate in all tracks to qualify for this final leaderboard
3. The lowest total time wins.
4. Micro Tier Scoring System - CASH prizes for 777 Bonus Prize Tier Members!
1. A score based on rank within your micro tier is given
2. You must participate in the previous track to be assigned a micro tier
3. The worst micro tier rank is dropped
4. The lowest total score wins
5. Ties broken by average tier placement
