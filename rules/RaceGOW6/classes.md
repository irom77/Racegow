RaceGOW Basic Concept:

RaceGOW is a series of “mini UTT” tracks designed to be small enough to be built in most homes. All RaceGOW tracks will fit in a 4’ x 6’ rectangle (if you are using the minimum gate size of 24”) but you will need some additional space around the outside of that to fly the tracks optimally. You will need to build the tracks according to the video and diagram and fly them as shown in the flythrough video. Your goal is to get your fastest 3 consecutive lap time and submit clip and time via the website: RaceGOW.com

If you are using PVC for gates you will need the following to build the RaceGOW tracks:
20 - sections of PVC (24” min gate size center to center, no maximum size but all your gates must be the same size. You must scale the entire track up equally based on your gate size)
4 - elbow connectors
6 - Tee connectors
6 - 4-Way Connectors
2 - 5-Way Connectors
(the higher direction connectors are not required but make some connections easier)

Classes & Whoop Requirements

RaceGOW Classes: What drones are allowed?

The Main Leaderboard is Open Class sponsored by HQProp:
1. Any Quad!
2. No size limit, no battery cell limit, no ducts required.
3. You must be able to record your DVR and/or the full flight area LOS with a timer in view. An OSD timer is strongly encouraged and recommended but not absolutely necessary**
4. Sizing up your gates and flying with a larger drone and bigger gates outside is allowed
5. RaceGOW is not responsible for damages if you think flying 5” 6S inside is a good idea

EMAX Class sponsored by EMAX USA: (track by track)
1. Any EMAX drone counts!
2. You can use any props, any camera, and any type of VTX that you choose.

EMAX Aero Whooptopia Spec sponsored by EMAX USA: (season long)
1. Must have a fully stock Aero BNF
2. Only modification allowed is the battery plug

Velocidrone Class:
Any drone in available in Velocidrone
1. Must participate on the official track
2. You do not need to submit a video for this class.

Common Questions

Can I participate in more than one class?

Yes, you are allowed to participate in all the classes. If you use a Nanoscout only you will be listed on the Main Leaderboard and the EMAX Nanoscout Spec Class Leaderboard. You do NOT have to submit two different runs. You DO NOT need to include a clip of your Velocidrone run, as long as you are on the official leaderboard at the deadline of the track you will be listed on the Velocidrone Leaderboard.
